\begin{cnabstract}
作为一种承载纽结型自旋纹理的三维拓扑准粒子，磁性霍普夫子（Hopfion）兼具稳健的拓扑保护与丰富的运动自由度，被视为高密度信息存储与类脑计算器件的候选载体。本研究借助微磁学仿真平台 Mumax3，围绕 Hopfion 在几类代表性磁体中的稳态存在条件，以及磁振子激励下的输运规律，开展系统考察。

就拓扑稳定性而言，本文以局域旋转矩阵结合环面坐标映射为数学框架，实现了任意拓扑荷数与反铁磁交替背景下 Hopfion 自旋态的解析化生成及三维可视化工具链。对以 FeGe 为代表、含体积型 Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya 相互作用（DMI）的铁磁体系，仿真结果归纳出维持 Hopfion 稳态需同时满足的三项要件——解析初始构型、边界层冻结处理以及退磁场纳入。当切换至竞争交换作用铁磁体系时，观测到 Hopfion 收敛至一个固有尺寸吸引子，对应大半径 $R_{\text{eq}} = \SI{2.60}{nm}$；通过控制变量式数值实验可以排除背景磁化取向对漂移行为的作用。针对各向异性参数的精细化扫描将临界失稳阈值定位于 $K_{u1,c} \in (\num{52e3}, \num{55e3}) \, \si{J/m^3}$ 区间。

进一步地，本研究在同一竞争交换模型中系统分析了 Hopfion 受自旋波激励后的响应规律。数值证据表明，仅当磁振子沿面内方向极化时，Hopfion 才会被有效驱动；面外极化分量对平动几乎毫无贡献。从频谱扫描结果看，轴向传播模式下出现多个共振峰，其中 $\SI{1100}{GHz}$ 附近响应最为强烈，对应位移 $\SI{18.1}{nm}$；漂移速度随激励幅度近似呈 $v \propto B_0^{1.99}$ 的幂律关系。而面内传播激励则驱使 Hopfion 以接近 $90°$ 的拓扑霍尔角偏移，该偏转角度在改变频率、强度与激励几何后依然稳健。需要指出的是，上述观测在微磁学尺度上首次定量刻画了 Hopfion 的磁振子霍尔效应。本文据此挖掘面内与轴向两种传播模式的方向互补特性，设计并通过仿真验证了借助频率切换实现 Hopfion 轴向双向可逆输运的调控策略。

在上述结果基础上，本文凝练出 Hopfion 所具备的激发阈值、频率选择响应以及方向编码能力等动力学指纹，将之与生物神经元的关键功能进行映射，进而勾勒出一种以自旋波为输入媒介的 Hopfion 类脑器件概念架构。

\cnkeywords{霍普夫子；微磁学模拟；拓扑稳定性；自旋波驱动；磁振子霍尔效应；神经形态计算}
\end{cnabstract}

\begin{enabstract}
Magnetic hopfions are 3D topological magnetic quasi-particles with knot-like spin configurations, exhibiting strong topological stability and multiple dynamical modes. They hold great promise for high-density storage and neuromorphic computing. Using Mumax3, we investigated the stability conditions and spin-wave-driven dynamics of hopfions in different magnetic systems.

First, based on existing toroidal coordinate mapping theories, we developed a programmatic initialization script and 3D visualization tools for hopfions with arbitrary topological charges and antiferromagnetic backgrounds. In a FeGe-based ferromagnetic system with bulk Dzyaloshinskii-Moriya interaction (DMI), we identified three necessary conditions for hopfion stability: analytical initial state, boundary layer freezing, and demagnetization effects. In a frustrated ferromagnetic system, we found an intrinsic size attractor ($R_{\text{eq}} = \SI{2.60}{nm}$). Background magnetization orientation showed no influence on hopfion drift. The critical anisotropy threshold was determined as $K_{u1,c} \in (\num{52e3}, \num{55e3}) \, \si{J/m^3}$.

Second, we studied spin-wave-driven hopfion dynamics in the frustrated exchange system. Only in-plane oscillating spin waves effectively drive hopfions. Frequency sweeps revealed a multi-peak resonance spectrum, with the strongest response at $\SI{1100}{GHz}$ under axial propagation. The velocity scales as $v \propto B_0^{1.99}$ with driving amplitude. In-plane propagation produces a stable topological Hall angle of $\sim 90°$, providing the first micromagnetic quantitative verification of the magnonic Hall effect for hopfions. Additionally, we proposed a bidirectional axial control scheme via spin-wave frequency switching.

Finally, exploiting the threshold effect, frequency selectivity, and directional encoding of hopfion dynamics, we mapped these behaviors to neuron functionalities and proposed a conceptual spin-wave-based hopfion neuromorphic device.

\enkeywords{hopfion; micromagnetic simulation; topological stability; spin-wave driving; magnonic Hall effect; neuromorphic computing}
\end{enabstract}
